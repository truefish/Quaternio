Quaternio bundles FFmpeg and ffprobe as separate executable programs.
FFmpeg project and source: https://ffmpeg.org/ and https://git.ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg.git

See provenance.json for the exact download URLs, archive SHA256 digests,
upstream version/source information, and hashes of the packaged binaries.
The *-version.txt, *-configuration.txt and *-license.txt files are captured
from BOTH packaged programs on the native release runner. They describe
the actual build, including enabled external libraries and license version.
Full GPL v2/v3 texts are included in licenses/ (from official FFmpeg n7.1.1
COPYING.GPLv2 and COPYING.GPLv3). The applicable version is reported by -L.
The BtbN archive's LICENSE.txt is also preserved when supplied.
Quaternio's own LICENSE does not replace third-party licenses.

These are checksum-pinned third-party binaries, not reproducible source builds.
A digest fixes the accepted archive bytes; it does not guarantee continued
hosting, source availability, or byte-identical rebuilds. macOS ad-hoc signing
can change binary bytes; provenance records the final packaged binary hashes.

Builder/source references:
BtbN: https://github.com/BtbN/FFmpeg-Builds
BtbN dependencies/build recipes: https://github.com/BtbN/FFmpeg-Builds/tree/master/scripts.d
OSXExperts: https://www.osxexperts.net/ (build instructions and source links)

These builder links are informational and may change. They are NOT an archive
of exact corresponding source for these binaries and their linked dependencies,
and this notice is NOT a written source offer. Exact dependency source revisions,
patches, and a complete corresponding source bundle are not available in this
repository and have not been independently established for these builds.
Native CI workers download and inspect these binaries; they do not build FFmpeg
from source and do not establish source-build reproducibility.
The distribution maintainer still
needs to obtain, retain, and provide corresponding source, patches, build scripts,
and dependency licenses as required for the selected builds. Including these
notices alone does not establish complete license compliance.
